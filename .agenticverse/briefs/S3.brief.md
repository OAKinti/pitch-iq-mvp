Owner decision D-9B (Nov 24): squads are invite-only. A member creates an invite link and shares it; opening the link joins the squad. A friend-list join comes later, when friends exist.

## Server (already committed; read it): `supabase/migrations/004_security_hardening.sql`

- Direct inserts into `group_members` are rejected; the group owner is auto-added on group creation.

## Behaviour / clauses
- INV-1 `parseInviteUrl` accepts only the app scheme + `join` path + a valid code; rejects http(s) links, other paths, bad codes (no open-redirect, no arbitrary group ids in URLs).
- INV-2 Opening a join link while onboarding is incomplete stores it as `pendingInvite` and joins after onboarding + session.
- INV-3 Join errors map to a user-facing `group-join-notice` (under-13: "A parent needs to verify your account before you can join a squad."); never throws into render.
- INV-4 Under-13 without parental verification: the invite button is hidden too (they cannot share invites either) - consistent with SEC-17.
- INV-5 The URL listener is removed on unmount; one join per received URL.
